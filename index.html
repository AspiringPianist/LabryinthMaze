  • End

Keys

  • 1 2 3 or M switch view
  • W S walk, A D turn, F G strafe
  • Play: click to capture the mouse, Esc releases it, R levels the view
  • Q E look up and down, C V tilt
  • ↑ ↓ change speed, ← → scale an obstacle
  • x y z rotate one way, X Y Z the other
  • H show or hide the axes, Delete removes an obstacle

Mouse

  • Left drag moves the selected object in a design view
  • Right drag is the virtual trackball
  • In world view, right drag on empty space orbits the camera
  • In play view, click the maze and move the mouse to look. Esc releases it
  • The wheel scales the selected obstacle, or changes speed while playing
