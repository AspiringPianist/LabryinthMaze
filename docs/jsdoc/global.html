rebuildPlayerQuat()

Rebuilds the view quaternion from yaw, pitch, and roll. Yaw is around world Z, then pitch around Y, then roll around X. Rebuilding each time keeps the horizon level. Multiplying onto the old quaternion made later turns inherit the tilt, which rolled the view.
